Dashboard

See which ad paid you, not which ad got clicked.

Three numbers tell you if the account is healthy: revenue, blended ROAS and marketing efficiency. You'll know where you stand before your first meeting.

  • Revenue from your real sales, not what the platform claims
  • A briefing each morning on what moved yesterday
  • First click, last click or linear, in one tap
Leak Alerts

Spot a slipping campaign the next morning, not at month-end.

Campaigns ranked on revenue, not clicks. When one slips under the ROAS floor you set, it turns red and it's in the next morning's briefing, while there's still budget left to save.

  • Spend, revenue and ROAS side by side
  • Alert rules you set, per account
  • Today's leads by the hour, so a stall shows early
Lead Journey

Follow every lead from the ad to the payment.

Ad, form, WhatsApp chat, sales call, payment. Every step in one line, and the money lands on the ad that started it.

  • Score, stage and channels for every lead
  • Time from first touch to payment
  • Every payment tied back to the campaign
Growth Pulse

See what each ad really added.

Every sale traced back to its campaign, ad set and ad, so you can judge each one on what it brought in.

  • Cost per sale next to spend and revenue
  • Drill from campaign down to the single ad
  • Tracking issues flagged on the row they affect
Market Intelligence

Know what your competitors are running.

How many ads they're running, how that shifts day to day, and your share of voice against them.

  • Active ad volume, you against each competitor
  • Share of voice and engagement benchmarks
  • Filter by Meta, Google or LinkedIn

Meta and Google get your closed deals and their value, so their algorithms go looking for buyers, not form-fillers.

  • You choose which events to send, and their value
  • Upload sales you closed over the phone
